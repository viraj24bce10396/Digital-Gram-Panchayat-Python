# Presentation Outline: Digital Gram Panchayat

## Slide 1 — Title
**Digital Gram Panchayat**
**Smart Digital Governance for Rural Communities**

- Student Name
- Department / Course
- Year / Semester

## Slide 2 — Problem Statement
- Traditional governance systems rely on manual paperwork
- Citizens face long delays and confusion
- Application tracking is inefficient
- Lack of transparency and accountability
- Service delivery is fragmented

## Slide 3 — Solution
- Digital platform for public services
- Centralized data and workflow management
- Role-based access for citizens, staff, and admin
- Transparent service processing
- Reduced paperwork and faster approvals

## Slide 4 — Objectives
- Digitize village-level services
- Improve transparency
- Reduce administrative delays
- Support citizen-friendly governance
- Build a scalable e-governance platform

## Slide 5 — Key Features
- User registration and login
- Service catalog
- Application submission
- Status tracking
- Admin dashboard
- Staff workflow management
- Notifications and updates

## Slide 6 — System Workflow
1. Citizen registers
2. Selects service
3. Submits request
4. Staff reviews request
5. Status is updated
6. Citizen tracks result

## Slide 7 — Roles and Access
### Citizen
- Apply for services
- View profile
- Track requests

### Staff
- Review applications
- Approve or reject
- Add notes

### Admin
- Manage services
- Monitor platform performance
- Oversee workflow

## Slide 12 — Conclusion
Digital Gram Panchayat is a practical and socially relevant e-governance solution that applies modern web technologies to improve village administration and public service delivery. It demonstrates how digital transformation can make governance more transparent, efficient, and citizen-friendly.
